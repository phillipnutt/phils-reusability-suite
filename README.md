# Phil's Reusability Suite

> **Written by an AI.** Every line of code in this mod was written by Claude (Anthropic's AI model).
> Phil Nutt designs it and play-tests it. He isn't a programmer, and no human has reviewed the code.
> Back up your saves before trying it.

A Kerbal Space Program 1.12 career mod that makes ships things you build, fly, bring home and fly again:

- **A build queue.** Ships take time to build, scaled by their price. Pay for overtime to build faster,
  or outsource to have it now.
- **A hangar.** Recovered ships are stored exactly as they came back and fly again after a cheap refit.
- **Stage recovery.** Boosters that would survive their landing come home to the hangar.
- **Part reuse.** Loose recovered parts make new builds cheaper and faster.
- **A flown booster under a new payload.** Pick a stored first stage in the VAB; only the new parts cost
  full price and full build time.

It needs Phil's Builds, and uses its families, marks and launcher ratings.

**Status: in development.** Nothing here is ready to play yet. The design is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).
